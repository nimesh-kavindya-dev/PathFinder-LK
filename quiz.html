Interactive IT Career Match Quiz

Unsure whether your natural talents lean towards Software Engineering, Data Analysis, Data Engineering, Cybersecurity, or UI/UX Design? Answer 9 self-reflection questions to receive a tailored recommendation.

How It Works

  • Select the answer that most genuinely reflects your natural study interests and problem-solving preferences.
  • There are no right or wrong answers; each choice highlights alignment with distinct IT disciplines.
  • Takes approximately 3–4 minutes to complete. All 9 questions are required for an accurate score.
