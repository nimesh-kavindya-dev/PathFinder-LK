Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from first-year and second-year Sri Lankan computing undergraduates about internships, portfolios, and choosing an IT specialization.

Many students begin exploring Software Engineering or Data Analytics in Year 1 and later specialize in Cloud & DevOps, Data Engineering, or QA Automation during Year 2 or Year 3.

No. Technical interviewers in Sri Lanka prioritize your fundamental understanding, problem-solving ability, and 2–3 well-documented projects on your GitHub or Behance/Figma portfolio far above paid online course certificates.

Start preparing your GitHub portfolio and CV at least 4 to 6 months before your university's official industrial placement semester. Completing Stages 1 through 5 of your chosen PathFinder LK roadmap will put you ahead of most internship applicants.

PathFinder LK includes dedicated non-coding and hybrid tracks: Business Analyst & Project Manager, Product Manager, Digital Marketing & Growth Strategist, Financial Analyst & FinTech Specialist, UI/UX Designer, and Creative Multimedia & Motion Designer—each with its own 6-stage roadmap and portfolio project guide.

Quality beats quantity. Aim for 2 to 3 original, well-documented projects with clean README files, case studies, or live demos rather than 10 unfinished tutorial clones. Check our Career Details page for specific project ideas for each of the 15 roles.
